  reflect the host's system-dark preference and prove the matching dark tokens.

  store screenshots. Real screenshots replace them only after running products
  and explicit store-art approval.
- The beta form routes to a local acknowledgement page and does not transmit an
  email address. Postmark capture remains behind provider and privacy approval.
- The temporary lettermark is not a final app icon. Icon and store artwork work
  remains gated on explicit visual approval.
