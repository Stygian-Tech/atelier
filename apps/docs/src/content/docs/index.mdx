:::danger[Public PDS records are readable]
Until Permissioned Spaces support is available and enabled, first-party Notes, Tasks, Calendar, Project, Capture, and relationship records written to a standard ATProto PDS are public. Provider credentials and provider content are not written there by default.
:::
